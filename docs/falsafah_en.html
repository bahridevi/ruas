Open icon philosophy · Draft 0.1 · 15 October 2026 · Bahasa Indonesia

Ruas.

A free public icon set that grows like bamboo: light because it is hollow, strong because it is jointed, flexible enough for every need, growing in clumps, and belonging to everyone.

Ruas is Indonesian for the segment of a bamboo stem between two joints. The six principles keep their Indonesian names, with English beside them.

00

Core: why start from a philosophy

Every Ruas icon must answer one question: is it light, orderly, flexible, honest in meaning, part of the family, and for everyone?

Icon sets that last have written rules. SF Symbols and Material Symbols are guarded by very detailed guidelines. A set that relies only on its designer's taste stops growing when the designer gets busy. Feather stalled at around 280 icons, and in 2020 the community continued it as Lucide.

The philosophy here works as a fence. Every principle has a measurable drawing rule and a way to test it, so the 100th contributor produces icons as clean as the first. Rules a machine can check are checked by a machine.

02

Bamboo Philosophy: six traits, six principles

Bamboo is light because it is hollow, strong because it is jointed, flexible in the wind, grows straight, lives in clumps, and is used by everyone, from homes to mosques. Each trait becomes a principle that can be tested.

1. Berongga · Hollow LIGHT

As few strokes as possible. Anything that does not help people recognize the icon is removed.

Rule
At most 4 elements per icon. Average file ≤ 400 bytes, largest 1 KB. No filter, mask, or gradients. Coordinates use at most 2 decimals.
Test
Machine: an automatic checker on every contribution.

2. Beruas · Jointed ORDERLY

Every bamboo joint is evenly spaced. Every icon stands on the same grid.

Rule
Canvas 24, live area 20. Line angles 0°, 45°, 90° (30° and 60° when needed). Gap between lines at least 2 px. Points on multiples of 0.25.
Test
Machine: checks area bounds, coordinate multiples, and gaps.

3. Lentur · Flexible ONE SOURCE, MANY NEEDS

One drawing serves every size, weight, and color mode.

Rule
Drawn as centerlines, readable at weights 1 to 2.5. Every part has a role: body, detail, or accent. That is what makes the Layer, Accent, and Fill modes possible without redrawing.
Test
Automatic contact sheet: 16/24/48 px × weight 1/1.5/2.5 × 4 modes.

4. Lurus · Straight HONEST MEANING

Bamboo grows straight. One icon carries one meaning that is understood at once.

Rule
Use objects people know today. Conventions that are already global stay (a floppy disk means save). No letters inside icons except currency symbols. No brand logos.
Test
Human: a 5-second recognition test with 10 people; passes when ≥ 8 guess correctly.

5. Berumpun · Clumping ONE FAMILY

No single stem stands out on its own. A new icon must look like a sibling.

Rule
Must carry the Leaf Corner signature when it has cornered planes. Visual weight matches its neighbors. Names follow the standard pattern.
Test
Human: placed among 8 random icons, then reviewed by 2 people.

6. Untuk semua · For everyone INCLUSIVE

Bamboo is used in palaces and in villages. Ruas is free for anyone to use.

Rule
MIT with no attribution required. Names and tags in two languages (ID and EN). Accessible labels. Human figures are neutral and faceless. Symbols of every religion in Indonesia are crafted to the same standard. Directional icons have a mirrored version.
Test
The accessibility checklist in chapter 09.
03

Signature: Sudut Daun (Leaf Corner)

Correction to the first idea: the "breathing gap" (broken line) turned out to be used already by the Broken style in Solar Icons. Gaps also disappear in the Fill variant and can change meaning, for example on a power icon or an open padlock. So gaps are not the signature, and the idea of "breath" moved to motion.

The rules

  • The top-left and bottom-right corners curve wide (R 4). The top-right and bottom-left corners are sharp (r 1). The shape recalls a bamboo leaf tapering at both ends, with the top-right tip as the direction of growth.
  • Applies to every cornered plane: documents, screens, cards, chat bubbles, calendars, padlocks.
  • R may not exceed half the shortest side. Small planes (< 8 px) use R 2 and r 0.5.
  • Circles stay perfect and carry no signature.
  • In the mirrored (RTL) version, the Leaf Corner is redrawn in its standard direction. The signature does not flip.

Why the Leaf Corner was chosen

"Unused" means not found in any large UI icon set. This must be rechecked before launch.

Second differentiator: Aksen Makna (Meaning Accent)

The second color is only for the part that changes when the meaning changes: the + on add user, the dot on a bell, the shackle on a padlock, the arrow on download, the needle on qibla. Icons without such a part (search, document, home) get no accent. Accent is never forced, and it must never be the only carrier of meaning, because the shape must stay complete for people with color blindness.

06

Color modes

Four modes, equivalent to the SF Symbols rendering modes but for the open web. All modes come from one drawing because every part already has a role (body, detail, accent).

ModeHow it worksUsed for
Line (Garis)One color, currentColorNavigation, buttons, table content
Layer (Lapis)Body filled with 16% of the main colorEmpty states, feature cards, small illustrations
Accent (Aksen)Meaningful part uses a second colorNotifications, status, primary actions
Fill (Isi)Solid body, details knocked outThe active tab or menu item
 
/* Set through CSS, without touching the icon files */
.ruas {
  color: #17211A;           /* main color */
  --ruas-aksen: #C27600;    /* accent color */
  --ruas-lapis: .16;        /* Layer mode opacity */
  --ruas-tebal: 1.5;        /* stroke weight */
}

The default accent (kunyit, turmeric) is tested at a minimum of 3:1 against light and dark backgrounds, per WCAG 1.4.11 for non-text elements.

07

Breathing motion

Motion replaces the "breathing gap" as the form of breath. Each accented icon has one motion born from its meaning, and only the accent part moves. The color rule and the motion rule form one system.

  • Pulse (denyut) for new status: the bell dot, the date on a calendar, the + sign.
  • Drop (turun) for progress: the download arrow.
  • Typing (ketik) for ongoing activity: the dots in a chat bubble.
  • Open (buka) for status change: the padlock shackle.
  • Seek (cari) for direction finding: the qibla needle.

Rules: 200–600 ms for one-shot motion. Looping motion only for ongoing status. Turns off automatically when the device asks for reduced motion (prefers-reduced-motion). Motion is pure CSS, no animation library.

Accessibility & language
  • Text alternatives (WCAG 1.1.1): a component without a label is automatically aria-hidden (decorative). With a label, it becomes role="img" and uses aria-label.
  • Contrast (1.4.11): default palette ≥ 3:1. Accent must never be the only distinction.
  • Touch target (2.5.8): default size 24 px.
  • Motion: follows prefers-reduced-motion.

Naming and search

Code names use English in the object-modifier pattern (user-plus, bell-ring) so they feel familiar to programmers everywhere. Every icon carries an Indonesian name, tags, and synonyms. Typing "cart" or the Indonesian "keranjang" finds cart; typing "bell" or "lonceng" finds bell.

10

Tech & distribution
  1. Single source: one SVG file per icon, parts marked by role (body, detail, accent), plus one metadata file (ID/EN names, tags, category, mirrorable, motion).
  2. Philosophy guard: an automatic checker for viewBox, element count, decimals, file size, embedded colors (currentColor required), and live-area bounds. Submissions that fail cannot be merged.
  3. Build: SVG optimization, then output to @ruas/react, vue, svelte, web components, SVG sprite, CDN, and a Figma library. All tree-shakable, SSR-safe, with zero runtime dependencies.
  4. Site: controls like chapter 04, copy SVG/JSX/Vue, PNG download at 1×/2×/3×, and links that keep your settings.
// Planned usage (not released yet)

Versioning: semver. Renaming an icon is a major version, and the old name stays as an alias for one major version. Every release comes with a changelog.

11

Governance
  • MIT license: free to use, modify, and sell with no attribution required, just like Lucide and Phosphor.
  • Contribution flow: request, then sketch, philosophy test (machine and human), review by 2 people, then release.
  • Changing the philosophy happens only through a written proposal (RFC) that explains the problem and its impact on existing icons, never out of taste.
  • Sustainability: open donations through GitHub Sponsors or Open Collective. Icons are never locked behind payment.

Rules

  • One primary category per icon, chosen by where people are most likely to look for it, not by the object. A calculator goes in keuangan, not perangkat.
  • At most 2 extra categories (juga), so an icon appears in other fields without being duplicated.
  • Series are markers that cross categories. Nusantara icons stay in the field that matches their function: becak in transportasi, bedug in keagamaan. Traditional houses go in bangunan with series rumah-adat.
  • Humanities is not a category of its own, because it is too abstract to draw. History, language, art, and philosophy go in pendidikan (Education & Humanities). Psychology goes in kesehatan (Health & Psychology): brain, psychological tests, counseling, mood.
  • Religions are equal: every religion in Indonesia gets the same basic set (house of worship, scripture, symbol, holy day).
  • New categories only come through a written proposal (RFC). The philosophy guard rejects categories that are not on the list.
